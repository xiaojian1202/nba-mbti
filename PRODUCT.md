# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript app keeps the approved browser-only first release lightweight.

## Users

Basketball players and NBA fans who want to explore their own on-court playing style.

## Product Purpose

A basketball-themed personality quiz where players answer game situations and receive a named on-court archetype.

## Operating Context

Someone can take the quiz on a phone or desktop browser and share a result link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The first release has 46 items across nine playing-style traits and 72 archetypes. It takes about ten minutes. It does not use accounts, collect answers, or compare users to real NBA players.

## Product Principles

- Make basketball situations concrete and familiar.
- Make every result feel like a plausible way to help a team.
- Keep the quiz quick enough to finish in one sitting.
